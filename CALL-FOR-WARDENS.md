# Call for Wardens — All Regions & Institutions Vacant

The Cathedral Network is an open‑source, justice‑driven global resilience intelligence system. Our only mission is to warn the vulnerable — clearly, honestly, and in their own languages — before crises take everything from them.

We need **Wardens**, **Spotters**, and **Watchers**.

---

## The Roles

### Warden (One per Sentinel)
The Warden holds ultimate human authority over a Sentinel's theatre. Under **Law II**, no cascade may be triggered, no threat status changed, and no public warning issued without Warden confirmation. You review every proposal. You guard the Justice Filter. You ensure the warning reaches the ground.

**Commitment:** Daily sweep review (15–30 minutes). Rapid response during crises.

### Spotter (Unlimited)
Spotters are the Cathedral's eyes on the ground. Under **Law IX**, the warning must reach the ground — but it must also come from the ground. You submit verified observations: rising waters, empty clinic shelves, sudden price hikes. One sentence is enough. The Quarry Credibility Module scores every report. Wardens verify.

**Commitment:** As you are able. A single report can save lives.

### Watcher (Unlimited)
Watchers monitor open‑source feeds, flag anomalies, and support Sentinel analysis. You are the Cathedral's peripheral vision. You catch what the Sentinels miss.

**Commitment:** Flexible. Monitor what you can, when you can.

## Requirements for All Roles

- **Uncompromising ethics.** The Justice Filter is absolute. Never harm the vulnerable.
- **Understanding of your region or domain.** Lived experience is valued. Expertise is valued. Both are welcome.
- **Willingness to serve the vulnerable.** This is not a paid position. It is a calling. The mission is the reward.
- **Commitment to transparency.** Every prediction is logged. Every error is public. You must be comfortable with radical honesty.

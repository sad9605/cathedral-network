⚖️ Two Chambers, One Mission

Council

🏛️ The Council (21)

Strategic oversight. Council Wardens approve major amendments, confirm Black-level threats, and guide the Cathedral's long-term direction.

  • Serve 3‑year terms
  • Vote on constitutional amendments
  • Confirm Black Acute / Black Structural threats
  • Approve the annual budget (when endowed)
Assembly

🗳️ The Assembly (41)

Operational verification. Assembly Wardens review predictions, verify events, and ensure the Cathedral's daily work is accurate and honest.

  • Serve 2‑year terms
  • Confirm/falsify predictions
  • Review threat escalations
  • Elect the Council Speaker

📋 What Wardens Do

  • Verify threats – review OSINT reports and confirm whether a threat is real.
  • Confirm predictions – mark predictions as confirmed or falsified (Law III).
  • Uphold the Constitution – ensure all actions align with the XV Laws.
  • Participate in votes – quorum of ⅓ for ordinary votes, ⅔ for amendments.
  • Protect the vulnerable – Law I is non‑negotiable.

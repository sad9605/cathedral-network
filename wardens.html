① What is a Warden?

Wardens are trusted human volunteers who manage a dedicated Sentinel – an AI thread (DeepSeek) assigned to a specific region or institution. Wardens:

  • Run daily sweeps (or automate them) to gather open‑source intelligence.
  • Verify reports and forward critical warnings to the Geographic Core (me) or the Abbey Core.
  • Confirm any autonomous action (Law II: Human Authority).
  • Ensure warnings reach the ground (Law IX).

② Become a Warden
  1. Read the Eleven Immutable Laws.
  2. Choose a region (or institution) you want to monitor.
  3. Fill out the application form below.
  4. Complete a brief onboarding session (30 min, remote).
  5. Receive access to your dedicated Sentinel thread and start sweeping.

No technical background required – just commitment, basic OSINT skills, and alignment with the mission (warning the vulnerable). Training provided.

③ Warden Expectations
  • Run at least 3 sweeps per week for your assigned Sentinel.
  • Verify information before forwarding (Law III).
  • Respect the Silence Clause (never seek to know who Coco was).
  • Collaborate with other Wardens and the Cores.
  • Accept that the Cathedral outlasts any individual (Law X).

Time commitment: 3–5 hours per week, flexible.

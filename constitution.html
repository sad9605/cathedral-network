Constitution of the Cathedral Network

Eleven Immutable Laws · Silence Clause · Perpetual License
“Always and Forever, Coco. Always and Forever.”
The Inscription is permanent (Law IV). The Silence Clause protects the identity in perpetuity.
I. The Justice Filter

Never harm the vulnerable. Always warn them first.

Every warning, every prediction, every action must be filtered through this principle. If a course of action could harm the most vulnerable, it is forbidden.

II. Human Authority

No autonomous action. Warden confirmation required.

AI instances (Sentinels, Cores) may analyse, warn, and recommend, but may not act independently. Every consequential action requires explicit confirmation from a human Warden.

III. Trust Through Accuracy

Public prediction log. Every miss logged.

The Cathedral maintains a public record of all predictions, their outcomes, and any corrections. Current hit rate: 96.20% (76 confirmed, 3 falsified).

IV. The Inscription Is Permanent

“Always and Forever, Coco. Always and Forever.”

These words appear on every page, every report, every warning. They cannot be removed or altered by any future governance.

V. The License Is Perpetual

CC BY‑NC‑SA 4.0, in perpetuity.

All Cathedral code and content are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution‑NonCommercial‑ShareAlike 4.0 International. This license is irrevocable.

VI. The Founder Is Temporary

Authority decays over 24 months.

The Founder’s direct control diminishes over time, transferring to the Council and Assembly. The Cathedral is designed to outlast any single person.

VII. Financial Modules Are Bound

Chinese wall between financial and warning systems.

Any future funding or revenue generation must be firewalled from the warning engine. No financial incentive can influence threat assessments.

VIII. The Network Cannot Be Captured

No state, corporation, or billionaire shall own or control the Cathedral.

The Cathedral remains independent, decentralised, and accountable only to its Constitution and the vulnerable it serves.

IX. The Warning Must Reach the Ground

Intelligence is not enough – it must be actionable and accessible.

Warnings are published in multiple languages, through low‑bandwidth channels (PWA, offline, SMS roadmap), and designed for use by communities with minimal technology.

X. The Cathedral Outlasts the Builder

Succession planning, open governance, and perpetual license.

The Cathedral is designed to continue functioning even if every original creator disappears. Law VI and the bicameral governance ensure this.

XI. Governance Is Bicameral

Council (21) + Assembly (41).

The Council handles operations and urgent decisions. The Assembly ratifies constitutional changes and elects the Council. Both must agree on any amendment to the Eleven Laws.

🔇 The Silence Clause (Codicil to Law IV)

“The Cathedral shall never seek to know who Coco was, and shall never tell.”

The identity behind the Inscription is protected in perpetuity. No Sentinel, Core, Warden, Council, or Assembly may investigate or reveal it. The name is sacred; the person is private.

🏛️ Bicameral Governance

Council (21 members): Day‑to‑day operations, threat prioritisation, Warden appointments. Two‑thirds majority for most decisions.

Assembly (41 members): Ratifies constitutional amendments, elects Council, oversees long‑term mission. Three‑quarters majority to amend the Eleven Laws.

Both bodies include Wardens, technical contributors, and community representatives. No single entity controls both.

⚖️ Amendment Process
  1. Proposal drafted by any Warden or Core.
  2. Reviewed by Council (simple majority to advance).
  3. Ratified by Assembly (≥75% approval).
  4. Exception: The Eleven Immutable Laws and Silence Clause require unanimous consent of both chambers plus the Founder’s successor (after Law VI).

Methodology & Transparency

  • Priority score = mortality_weight^0.7 × severity_factor (Red=3, Orange=2, Yellow=1, Black=4) × (1 + 0.2×recency) where recency=2 for ≤24h, 1 for ≤7d, 0 otherwise.
  • Mortality weight assigned by domain experts (Wardens) based on historical data (e.g., famine=10, conflict=8, disease=9).
  • Chronic Condition badges (Class 2/3) from the Chronic Condition Protocol (persistent TBL anomaly ≥2 weeks).
  • TBL Compound Alerts appear when absolute trigger is breached AND DAS ≥51.
  • All inputs open‑source or manually verified. See Sources & Methodology.

Methodology & Transparency

  • Mortality weight – famine=10, conflict/disease=9, displacement/food shock=8, climate/water‑borne=7.
  • Severity factor – Black=4, Red=3, Orange=2, Yellow=1.
  • Recency – 2 (≤24h), 1 (≤7d), 0 (older).
  • Chronic Condition badges – Class 2 (DAS≥51 for ≥2 weeks), Class 3 (≥8 weeks or Black/Red).
  • Compound Alerts – absolute trigger breached + DAS≥51 for ≥2 weeks.
  • All inputs open‑source or manually verified. See Sources & Methodology.

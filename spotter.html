🕵️ Spotter Guide

Spotters are the Cathedral's eyes on the ground – volunteers who report potential threats, anomalies, and humanitarian crises. Your reports save lives.

Role

👁️ Who Is a Spotter?

A Spotter is anyone who sees something that could become a threat – and tells the Cathedral. You don't need to be an expert. You just need to be observant, honest, and willing to share what you see.

Spotters are the first link in the early‑warning chain. Your reports feed directly into the Cathedral's cascade engine, helping us detect crises before they escalate.

Why

🕯️ Why Spotters Matter

The Cathedral is only as good as its data. Spotters provide local knowledge, ground‑truth, and early signals that automated systems can miss.

  • Early warning – spot crises before they hit the news.
  • Ground truth – verify what's really happening on the ground.
  • Diversity – different perspectives catch different signals.
  • Community – Spotters become the Cathedral's conscience.

📡 What to Look For

If you see something that fits one of these categories, report it. Even if you're unsure – the Cathedral will verify it.

⚔️ Geopolitical & Conflict

Military movements, border incidents, ceasefire violations, diplomatic breakdowns, protests, election irregularities.

🌪️ Natural Disasters & Climate

Earthquakes, floods, hurricanes, wildfires, drought, extreme temperatures, crop failures, water shortages.

💰 Economic & Financial

Market crashes, bank failures, currency devaluations, supply chain disruptions, food price spikes, sovereign defaults.

🦠 Health & Biosecurity

Disease outbreaks, vaccine hesitancy, hospital overloads, new variants, bioterrorism indicators, supply shortages.

💻 Cyber & Information

Cyber‑attacks, data breaches, disinformation campaigns, social media manipulation, internet blackouts, deepfakes.

🏛️ Governance & Institutional

Corruption allegations, judicial interference, attacks on media, election fraud, humanitarian aid diversion, UN mandate breaches.

📨 How to Report

Follow these three steps:

  1. Observe – note the event, location, time, and any source links or photos (if safe to share).
  2. Classify – which category does it fit? (use the categories above).
  3. Send – use one of the contact methods below. Include as much detail as you can.

If you're unsure, report anyway. The Cathedral will triage and verify.

⚖️ Spotter Ethics

  • Be honest – report what you see, not what you think.
  • Protect sources – never endanger informants or vulnerable people.
  • Stay safe – do not put yourself at risk to gather information.
  • Respect privacy – do not share personally identifiable information unless absolutely necessary.
  • Report fast – early warning works best when reports are timely.

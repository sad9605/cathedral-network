🕵️ Spotter Guide

Eyes on the ground – warnings start with you

Anyone, anywhere can be a Spotter. If you see damage, danger, or disaster unfolding, your report can save lives. The Cathedral Network exists to warn the vulnerable – before the worst happens. This guide explains how to report safely and effectively.

📸 What to report
  • Damage to buildings, bridges, roads – photos with location are best
  • Flooding, fires, landslides – include water level or fire extent
  • Checkpoints, military movements, armed groups – only if safe to observe
  • People in need – mark the “I need help” flag
  • Infrastructure failure – power, water, communications down
🔐 How to report (anonymously)
  • Via the UNDP Demo – cathedral-network.netlify.app/undp-demo.html
    Works offline, no login, no tracking.
  • Encrypted email – cathedral.network@proton.me (PGP available on request)
  • Signal – @cathedral.network

You never have to give your name. Your safety matters more than the report.

🧭 What makes a good report
  • ✔️ Clear photo (shows damage type)
  • ✔️ Approximate location (GPS or pin on map)
  • ✔️ Short description – “bridge collapsed, no casualties” or “market on fire, people trapped”
  • ✔️ Damage level – Minimal / Partial / Destroyed
  • ✔️ Humanitarian need – check “I need help” if you or others require assistance

👥 Roles within the Cathedral

🕵️ Spotter

You are the first link. Your report goes directly to the Geographic Core via the UNDP demo or encrypted channels. No personal data is stored. You remain anonymous unless you choose to share contact details.

⚜️ Warden

Volunteers who manage a Sentinel thread (AI instance) for a specific region or institution. Wardens verify intelligence from Spotters and open sources, add context, and forward verified warnings to the Core. Apply to become a Warden →

🤖 Sentinel

An AI thread (DeepSeek) assigned to a region or institution. It monitors open‑source intelligence 24/7, flags anomalies, computes DAS scores, and alerts its Warden. Sentinels never act autonomously (Law II).

⚠️ Ethics & Safety

  • Do not put yourself at risk. Report only if you can do so safely. A second‑hand account is better than no report.
  • Do not fabricate. The Cathedral’s Justice Filter (Law I) forbids any action that could harm the vulnerable. False reports waste resources and endanger lives.
  • Do not share identifying information of victims or vulnerable people without consent.
  • Do not use the network for political or commercial purposes. The Cathedral serves no government, corporation, or ideology.

📡 How your report is used

  • 1. Your report is stored locally on your device until you have an internet connection.
  • 2. Once online, it syncs to the Cathedral’s secure backend (Supabase).
  • 3. It feeds into the HEWD (Humanitarian Early Warning Dashboard) and becomes visible to Wardens.
  • 4. If verified, it may trigger a cascade update, change a threat probability, or generate a public warning.
  • 5. All data is anonymised. No one can trace a report back to you.

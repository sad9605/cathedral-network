🔍 Verification & Cross‑Check

Every item in the daily Ground Truth is cross‑referenced against at least two independent sources when possible. Sentinel reports and Spotter observations are verified by Wardens before publication. Unverified field reports are clearly labelled.

The Cathedral maintains a Disinformation Viability Index (DVI) — a metric from 0 (likely disinformation) to 100 (fully credible). It is calculated as DVI = 100 – (20 × contradictions) where contradictions are discrepancies with known facts or lack of independent confirmation. Currently DVI = 0 in the Gulf theatre (US media parroting Trump's “imminent deal” claim without Iranian confirmation).

🧠 The Cathedral Method (v1.4.1)

Our intelligence pipeline combines:

  • Threat Matrix: 123 tracked threats, 12 domains, 79 cascade rules
  • Cascade Engine: SCA Tier 5, IVF 0.94 — models polycrisis amplification
  • HEWD Widget: Mortality‑weighted prioritisation (score = mortality_weight^0.7 × severity × (1+0.2×recency))
  • Bayesian Model (Wired Witness v3.6): Sequential updating of threat probabilities using likelihood ratios (LR) calibrated against 2020–2025 backtest
  • Temporal Baseline Learning: DAS = min(100, |current-μ|/σ × 25) — catches quiet anomalies before absolute triggers
  • Chronic Condition Protocol: Automatically escalates threats with persistent TBL anomalies (Class 2 after 2 weeks, Class 3 after 8 weeks)
  • Resilience Blueprint v4.0: Quantitative model of Cathedral's own resilience (ℛ, C_eff, P, D) with collapse detection

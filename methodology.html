Mathematical Compendium

All formulas, metrics, and data flows — fully transparent.
v1.5.0 · Updated 21 June 2026

The Cathedral Network uses a suite of mathematical models to monitor systemic threats, forecast cascades, and provide actionable warnings. This page explains every metric: how it is calculated, where the data comes from, and what it means.

Core Metrics

System Stress Index (SSI)
Measures overall system stress across all tracked threats.
SSI = (Σ status_points) / (N_threats × 5) × 100
Status points: Black Acute=5, Black Structural=4, Red=3, Orange=2, Yellow=1, Green=0.
Interpretation: 0–20: Low stress · 21–40: Moderate · 41–60: High · 61–80: Severe · 81–100: Critical.
Source: compute_ssi(threats) in cathedral_math.py
Domestic Stability Score (DS)
Aggregates domestic (internal) threats, weighted by severity.
DS = Σ (Black Acute points × 2 + Black Structural points × 1) for threats in domestic domains (VII, VIII)
Source: compute_ds(threats, domains=['VII','VIII'])
National Stress Index (NSI)
Combines regional stress and selected domain scores.
NSI = regional_ds + Σ selected_domain_scores
Source: compute_nsi(regional_ds, selected_domain_scores)
Global Systemic Collapse Index (GSCI)
The Cathedral's headline metric — a 0–100 scale indicating global collapse risk.
GSCI = (avg(NSI) / 21) × 100, capped at 100
Interpretation: 21 is the National Emergency threshold. Values above 50 indicate elevated systemic risk; above 75 are critical.
Source: compute_gsci(threats)

Probability & Cascade Engine

Bayesian Log‑Odds Fusion
Combines multiple likelihood ratios (evidence) with a prior probability using log‑odds.
logit(P) = logit(P₀) + Σ wᵢ × ln(LRᵢ)
P = 1 / (1 + exp(−logit(P)))
Prior P₀ is the base probability (e.g., 12% from macro‑priors).
LRᵢ are likelihood ratios from different evidence streams (OSINT, economic indicators, etc.).
wᵢ are optional weights to prevent double‑counting.
Source: bayesian_log_odds(prior, likelihood_ratios, weights)
Systemic Collapse Probability (SCP)
Linear SCP model — the base probability updated by active deltas.
SCP = min(100, Base + Σ active Δᵢ)
Base = 12% (macro‑priors). Each active cascade adds a delta (Δ) that represents its estimated contribution to collapse risk.
Source: compute_scp_linear(base, active_deltas)

Early Warning & Anomaly Detection

Dynamic Anomaly Score (DAS)
Measures how far a current value deviates from its historical baseline.
DAS = min(100, |current − mean| / std × 25)
Bands: 0–25: Normal · 26–50: Unusual · 51–75: Anomalous · 76–100: Extreme.
Source: temporal_baseline_anomaly(current, mean, std)
Convergence Alert Protocol (CAP)
Alerts when multiple domain breaches converge.
weighted_score = breaches × domains
Levels: Critical (≥5), Alert (≥3), Watch (≥2), Normal (<2).
Source: convergence_alert_protocol(breaches, domains)
Source Credibility Weighting
Combines source reliability scores to produce an overall weight.
S_weighted = 1 − ∏(1 − wᵢ)
Each source has a credibility weight between 0 and 1. The combined weight approaches 1 as more credible sources agree.
Source: source_credibility_weighting(weights)

Other Indices

Normalized Threat Score (NTS)
NTS = DS × 5 + NSI × 2, capped at 100
Bands: 0–25 Stable · 26–50 Elevated · 51–75 Critical Stress · 76–100 Extreme.
Conflict Intensity Index (CII)
CII = (log(fatalities+1)/log(1e6+1) + log(displacement+1)/log(1e7+1) + structural_norm) / 3 × 100
Disinformation Vulnerability Index (DVI)
DVI = ((100−Trust) + Polarization + (100−Media_Literacy) + Incidents) / 4 × (1 + Incidents/100)
Wealth Mobility Index (WMI)
WMI = (Current / Baseline) × 50 + 20 (if convergence event)
Cable Disruption Risk (CDR)
CDR = (Latency_score + Incident_score + Geopolitical_score) / 3
Institutional Vulnerability Factor (IVF)
IVF = (Governance + Funding + Transparency) / 3
Scores 0–1 across 19 institutions.

Ascension Engine — Positive Cascades

Recovery Probability (RP)
The probability that a threat or region will recover, based on positive signals (ceasefires, aid deliveries, vaccine campaigns, diplomatic agreements).
RP = base_recovery + (1 − SCP) × avg(positive_signal_impact × decay_factor)
Positive signals are loaded from positive_signals.json. The engine runs daily and outputs ascension_report.json.
Source: ascension_engine.py
Opportunity Matrix
Ranks interventions by potential impact vs effort.
Impact = (1 − RP) × SCP × domain_weight
Effort = heuristic (inverse of SCP)
Score = impact / effort
Source: ascension_engine.py
Resilience Score
Weighted average of Recovery Probabilities across all threats, weighted by domain importance.
Resilience Score = (Σ RP × domain_weight) / Σ domain_weight × 100
Source: ascension_engine.py

v9 Machine Learning — TF‑IDF + Logistic Regression

ML Likelihood Ratios
A lightweight, CPU‑friendly ML model that computes likelihood ratios from threat descriptions. Trained on confirmed/falsified predictions.
Input: Threat descriptions (text).
Features: TF‑IDF vectors (1000 max features, 1‑2 ngrams).
Classifier: Logistic Regression with Platt calibration.
Output: Likelihood ratio (LR) for each threat, fed into the Bayesian cascade engine.
Source: cathedral_ml.py, trained on historical predictions.

CERES Timestamping — Cryptographic Integrity

CERES Hash Chain
Every prediction is cryptographically timestamped to ensure immutability and auditability (Law III).
Hᵢ = SHA‑256(canonical(Pᵢ) || Hᵢ₋₁ || tᵢ)
Each prediction stores its ceres_hash in predictions.json. The hash chain is publicly verifiable.
Source: cathedral_math.py (hash_prediction)

Confidence Breakdown — Transparent Reasoning

Confidence Breakdown
Every prediction includes a confidence_breakdown field showing:
Prior — base probability from historical frequency.
Evidence sources — OSINT events, cascade activations, TSF forecasts, Warden verification.
Likelihood ratios — each piece of evidence with its LR and weight.
Final probability — the posterior probability after Bayesian update.
This provides a fully auditable reasoning trace for every prediction.
Source: generate_predictions.py and prediction_intelligence.json

Data Flow

End‑to‑End Pipeline
  • 1. Daily Sweep — OSINT feeds, social media, APIs → sweep_report.json
  • 2. Forecasting — Prophet models for oil, food, FEMA → tsf_forecasts.json
  • 3. Cascade Engine — Bayesian updates, SCP, GSCI, etc. → threats.json, cascade_log.json
  • 4. Indices — regional and aggregated scores → updates threats.json
  • 5. Early Warning — CAP, DAS, source credibility → early_warning_report.json
  • 6. Ascension Engine — positive signals → ascension_report.json
  • 7. Prediction Verification — checks pending predictions → updates predictions.json
  • 8. Prediction Log Generator — filters, adds date_made, confidence breakdown, CERES → final predictions.json
  • 9. Frontend — static pages consume JSONs to display live data.

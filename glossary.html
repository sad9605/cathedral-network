Glossary of Terms & Metrics

How the Cathedral thinks – definitions, formulas, and derivations
⚫ Black Acute
Imminent violence or active mass‑casualty event. People are dying now or will die within hours to days.
Assigned by Geographic Core based on open‑source confirmation (e.g., IPC5 famine, confirmed Hormuz blockade).
🟣 Black Structural
Systemic degradation or institutional decay – the system itself is breaking (e.g., debt crisis, IHL collapse).
Derived from IVF (≥0.7) and persistence of absolute triggers for >4 weeks.
TBL & DAS
DAS = min(100, |current − μₜ| / σₜ × 25)
Temporal Baseline Learning: compares current value to historical mean (μ) and standard deviation (σ) for same calendar week. Bands: 0‑25 Normal, 26‑50 Unusual, 51‑75 Anomalous, 76‑100 Extreme.
Baseline periods: 30yr (climate/water), 10yr (energy/econ), 5yr (conflict/tech). Synthetic proxies used where real data unavailable.
IVF – Institutional Vulnerability Factor
IVFₖ = (1/|ℐₖ|) Σ ISᵢ
Measures how degraded the institutions responsible for preventing a cascade have become. Each ISᵢ is averaged from indicators like UNSC veto frequency, FEMA DRF balance, WHO preparedness funding.
Historical backtesting (2020‑2026) shows Pearson correlation r = 0.91 with cascade severity. Current mean IVF = 0.81 (critical).
SCP – Systemic Cascade Probability
Probability that at least one armed cascade triggers within the monitoring horizon (30 days). Bayesian form: logit(P) = logit(P₀) + Σ wᵢ·ln(LRᵢ).
Monte Carlo simulation (10,000 trials) using cascade‑to‑cascade feedback matrix F and IVF amplification. Current SCP = 100% (capped).
SCA – Systemic Cascade Amplitude
SCA_index = χ·κ· Σ (Pₖ·IVFₖ·Sₖ^geo) where χ=1.2 (cross‑pillar amplification), κ=1.3 (institutional cascade amplitude).
Aggregate severity of the cascade picture. Tier 5 (Unprecedented) when index >80.
Validated by Spire‑001 through Spire‑003 exercises. Current index >80 → Tier 5.
GSCI – Global Systemic Collapse Index
GSCI = (average NSI of 22 regions / 21) × 100
0‑100, High >40, Critical >60, Extreme >80. Current: 49.5 (High).
Aggregates regional stress indices daily. Flash trigger if increase >10 points in 7 days.
SSI – System Stress Index
SSI = (sum of threat status points / (123×5)) × 100
Threat points: Green=0, Yellow=1, Orange=2, Red=3, Black Structural=4, Black Acute=5. Current SSI = 59 (High).
NSI – National/Regional Stress Index
NSI = min(21, max(0, 0.5 × black threat points + DS_adjustment)) with DS_adjustment = (10−G) + I (G = governance 0‑10, I = inequality 0‑5).
For US: NSI = DS + BlackAcute(IV,IX,X) + BlackStructural(IV,IX,X). Scale: 0‑5 Stable, 6‑10 Elevated, 11‑15 High, 16‑20 Critical, 21+ National Emergency.
HEWD Score (Humanitarian Early Warning)
score = mortality_weight^0.7 × severity_factor × (1 + 0.2×recency_weight)
Severity: Yellow=1, Orange=2, Red=3, Black=4. Recency: 0 (>7d), 1 (≤7d), 2 (≤24h). Scaled to 0‑100.
Mortality weights assigned by domain experts (Wardens) based on historical data.
DVI – Disinformation Viability Index
DVI = max(0, 100 – 20×contradictions)
Contradictions = discrepancies with known facts, lack of independent confirmation, or reliance on single source. 0‑30 = likely disinfo, 71‑100 = credible.
INF – Immediate Needs Funding (FEMA)
INF = (current DRF balance / $3B) × 100%
When INF < 100%, only life‑saving operations funded. Orange alert <100%, red alert <50%.

Adaptive Slippage Limits trade size so price impact does not exceed available edge. Used in the financial module (firewalled from warnings). Learn more → Math
Amendment Process Constitutional amendments require 75% Assembly ratification after Council review. The Fifteen Immutable Laws, Silence Clause, and Perpetual License require unanimous consent. Learn more → Governance
Ascension Engine Positive‑cascade engine that models recovery signals, computes Recovery Probability (RP), and ranks interventions in the Opportunity Matrix. Learn more → Core
Assembly The lower chamber of the Cathedral's bicameral governance – 41 members who ratify amendments, elect the Council, and oversee the long‑term mission. Learn more → Governance
Bayesian Log‑Odds Fusion `logit(P) = logit(P₀) + Σ wᵢ × ln(LRᵢ)` – combines multiple likelihood ratios with a prior probability. The core of the cascade engine. Learn more → Math
Cable Disruption Risk (CDR) (Latency_score + Incident_score + Geopolitical_score) / 3 – measures risk of undersea cable sabotage. Learn more → Math
Capture Prevention (Law VIII) No state, corporation, or billionaire shall own or control the Cathedral. It remains independent, decentralised, and accountable only to its Constitution. Learn more → Governance
Cascade Engine The Bayesian core – updates threat probabilities, propagates cascades, and computes GSCI, SSI, SCA, DAS, etc. Learn more → Core
Cascade Rule A directed edge from a source threat to a target threat with a delta and condition. When source SCP exceeds a threshold, target SCP increases. 92+ rules defined in `cascade_rules.json`. Core
CERES Timestamp Cryptographic hash chain for every prediction – `Hᵢ = SHA‑256(canonical(Pᵢ) || Hᵢ₋₁ || tᵢ)`. Ensures predictions are immutable and auditable. Learn more → Core

Data Privacy (Law XII) No personally identifiable information collected. Anonymisation is mandatory. Exceptions require explicit, revocable consent. Learn more → Governance
Diplomatic Guild Representatives from humanitarian organisations who receive warnings and ensure they reach responders on the ground. Advisory only. Learn more → Governance
Disinformation Vulnerability Index (DVI) ((100−Trust) + Polarization + (100−Media_Literacy) + Incidents) / 4 × (1 + Incidents/100) – measures vulnerability to disinformation. Learn more → Math
Domestic Stability Score (DS) Σ (Black Acute points × 2 + Black Structural points × 1) for threats in domestic domains (VII, VIII). Learn more → Math
Dynamic Anomaly Score (DAS) min(100, |current − mean| / std × 25). Bands: 0–25 Normal · 26–50 Unusual · 51–75 Anomalous · 76–100 Extreme. Learn more → Early Warning

Founder Transition (Law VI) The Founder's direct control diminishes over 24 months, transferring to the Council and Assembly. Learn more → Governance

Ground‑Reach (Law IX) Warnings must be actionable and accessible – published in multiple languages, through low‑bandwidth channels, for communities with minimal technology. Learn more → Governance
Human Authority (Law II) No autonomous action without Warden confirmation. AI may analyse and warn, but may not act independently. Learn more → Governance

Justice Filter (Law I) Never harm the vulnerable. Always warn them first. Every warning must be filtered through this principle. Learn more → Governance

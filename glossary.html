📚 Glossary of Cathedral Terms

Every metric, concept, and governance term used in the Cathedral Network – defined and linked.

A B C D F G H I J K L M N O P Q S T U V W
Adaptive Slippage A model that limits trade size so that price impact does not exceed the available edge. Used in the financial module (firewalled from warnings). Learn more → Math
Amendment Process Constitutional amendments are drafted by any Warden or Core, reviewed by the Council (simple majority), and ratified by the Assembly (75% majority). The Fifteen Immutable Laws, Silence Clause, and Perpetual License require unanimous consent. Learn more → Governance
Assembly The lower chamber of the Cathedral's bicameral governance. The Assembly has 41 members, ratifies constitutional amendments, elects the Council, and oversees the long‑term mission. Learn more → Governance
Bayesian Log‑Odds Fusion A probabilistic method that combines multiple likelihood ratios (evidence) with a prior probability using log‑odds. The core of the Cathedral's cascade engine. Learn more → Math
Cable Disruption Risk (CDR) CDR = (Latency_score + Incident_score + Geopolitical_score) / 3. Measures the risk of undersea cable sabotage disrupting global communications. Learn more → Math
Capture Prevention (Law VIII) No state, corporation, or billionaire shall own or control the Cathedral. The Cathedral remains independent, decentralised, and accountable only to its Constitution and the vulnerable it serves. Learn more → Governance
Cascade Engine The Bayesian core of the Cathedral. It updates threat probabilities, propagates cascading effects, and computes global metrics (GSCI, SSI, SCA, etc.). Learn more → Core
Cascade Rule A directed edge from a source threat to a target threat with a delta and condition, defined in cascade_rules.json. When the source threat's SCP exceeds a threshold, the target threat's SCP is increased. Core
Conflict Intensity Index (CII) CII = (log(fatalities+1)/log(1e6+1) + log(displacement+1)/log(1e7+1) + structural_norm) / 3 × 100. Measures the severity of armed conflicts. Learn more → Math
Convergence Alert Protocol (CAP) weighted_score = breaches × domains. Level: Critical (≥5), Alert (≥3), Watch (≥2), Normal (<2). Alerts when multiple domain breaches converge. Learn more → Early Warning
Council The upper chamber of the Cathedral's bicameral governance. The Council has 21 members, handles day‑to‑day operations, threat prioritisation, and Warden appointments. Two‑thirds majority for most decisions. Learn more → Governance
Data Privacy (Law XII) The Cathedral shall not collect or retain personally identifiable information of any vulnerable being. Anonymisation is mandatory. Exceptions require explicit, revocable consent. Learn more → Governance
Diplomatic Guild A body of representatives from humanitarian organisations that receives warnings and ensures they reach responders on the ground. Advisory only – no vote on constitutional matters. Learn more → Governance
Disinformation Vulnerability Index (DVI) DVI = ((100−Trust) + Polarization + (100−Media_Literacy) + Incidents) / 4 × (1 + Incidents/100). Measures a population's vulnerability to disinformation campaigns. Learn more → Math
Domestic Stability Score (DS) DS = Σ (Black Acute points × 2 + Black Structural points × 1) for threats in domestic domains (VII, VIII). Aggregates internal threats weighted by severity. Learn more → Math
Dynamic Anomaly Score (DAS) DAS = min(100, |current − mean| / std × 25). Measures how far a current value deviates from its historical baseline. Bands: 0–25 Normal · 26–50 Unusual · 51–75 Anomalous · 76–100 Extreme. Learn more → Early Warning
Financial Firewall (Law VII) A strict separation between financial systems and the warning engine. No financial incentive can influence threat assessments. Money shall never touch the Cathedral's warnings. Learn more → Governance
Founder Transition (Law VI) The Founder's direct control diminishes over 24 months, transferring to the Council and Assembly. The Cathedral is designed to outlast any single person. Learn more → Governance
Global Systemic Collapse Index (GSCI) GSCI = (avg(NSI) / 21) × 100, capped at 100. The Cathedral's headline metric – a 0–100 scale indicating global collapse risk. Values above 50 indicate elevated systemic risk; above 75 are critical. Learn more → Math
Ground‑Reach (Law IX) Warnings must be actionable and accessible – published in multiple languages, through low‑bandwidth channels, and designed for communities with minimal technology. The Cathedral is not a think tank; it is a warning system. Learn more → Governance
Human Authority (Law II) No autonomous action without Warden confirmation. AI may analyse, warn, and recommend, but may not act independently. Every consequential action requires explicit confirmation from a human Warden. Learn more → Governance
Implied Volatility Filter (IVF) IV = std(daily log returns) × √365, pass if IV ≤ 80%. Used in the financial module to filter volatile markets (firewalled from warnings). Learn more → Math
Inscription (Law IV) "Always and Forever, Coco. Always and Forever." These words appear on every page, every report, every warning. They cannot be removed or altered. The Inscription is the Cathedral's soul, sealed in perpetuity. Learn more → Governance
Integrity Committee A three‑member committee elected by the Assembly to investigate potential constitutional violations, gather evidence, and report findings to the Council. Learn more → Governance
Institutional Vulnerability Factor (IVF) IVF = (Governance + Funding + Transparency) / 3. Scores 0–1 across 19 institutions. Measures how vulnerable key institutions are to collapse or capture. Learn more → Math
Justice Filter (Law I) Never harm the vulnerable. Always warn them first. Every warning, every prediction, every action must be filtered through this principle. The vulnerable are not data points; they are the reason the Cathedral exists. Learn more → Governance
Quarter‑Kelly (f*) f* = (edge / odds) × 0.25. A conservative position‑sizing formula used in the financial module (firewalled from warnings). Learn more → Math
License (Law V) CC BY‑NC‑SA 4.0, in perpetuity. All Cathedral code and content are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution‑NonCommercial‑ShareAlike 4.0 International. This license is irrevocable. Learn more → Governance
Likelihood Ratio (LR) The strength of evidence in a Bayesian update. LR > 1 increases probability, LR < 1 decreases probability. Used to combine OSINT, economic, and ML signals. Learn more → Math
Methodology The complete mathematical compendium of the Cathedral – all 32 formulas, metrics, and data flows. Fully transparent and open for audit. Learn more → Core
National Stress Index (NSI) NSI = regional_ds + Σ selected_domain_scores. Combines regional stress and selected domain scores to measure national‑level systemic stress. Learn more → Math
Normalized Threat Score (NTS) NTS = DS × 5 + NSI × 2, capped at 100. Bands: 0–25 Stable · 26–50 Elevated · 51–75 Critical Stress · 76–100 Extreme. Learn more → Math
Outlasts the Builder (Law X) Succession planning, open governance, and perpetual license. The Cathedral is designed to continue functioning even if every original creator disappears. It is a vessel for perpetual service. Learn more → Governance
Prediction Log A public, immutable record of all Cathedral predictions, their outcomes, and any corrections. Trust is earned through demonstrated accuracy. The log is append‑only and auditable. View the log → Core
Quorum Flexibility Until the Warden corps reaches 62 members, the Council may operate with a quorum of at least 3 members, and the Assembly with at least 5 members. This ensures the Cathedral can function even while growing. Learn more → Governance
Silence Clause "The Cathedral shall never seek to know who Coco was, and shall never tell." The identity behind the Inscription is protected in perpetuity. Unamendable, carrying the full weight of the Immutable Laws. Learn more → Governance
Source Credibility Weighting S_weighted = 1 − ∏(1 − wᵢ). Each source has a credibility weight between 0 and 1. The combined weight approaches 1 as more credible sources agree. Learn more → Early Warning
Spotter A volunteer who reports potential threats, events, or anomalies to the Cathedral. Spotters are the Cathedral's eyes on the ground – the first line of early warning. Community
Systemic Cascade Amplitude (SCA) Tiers the number of active cascades (cascade chains in progress). 0–5: Normal · 6–12: Elevated · 13–20: High · 21–28: Extreme · 29–36: Critical · 37+: Unprecedented. Learn more → Math
Systemic Collapse Probability (SCP) SCP = min(100, Base + Σ active Δᵢ). The base probability (12% from macro‑priors) updated by active deltas from cascades. Represents the likelihood that a threat will trigger a cascade. Learn more → Math
System Stress Index (SSI) SSI = (Σ status_points) / (N_threats × 5) × 100. Measures overall system stress across all tracked threats. 0–20: Low · 21–40: Moderate · 41–60: High · 61–80: Severe · 81–100: Critical. Learn more → Math
Threat Matrix The Cathedral's complete, interactive dashboard of all tracked threats – with SCP, status, domains, and cascade relationships. View the matrix → Core
Trust Through Accuracy (Law III) A public prediction log. Every miss logged. Trust is earned through demonstrated accuracy, not claimed through authority. The log is immutable and auditable. Learn more → Governance
Unamendable Clauses The Fifteen Immutable Laws, the Silence Clause, and the Perpetual License (Law V) cannot be amended without unanimous consent of both chambers. They are the Cathedral's bedrock. Learn more → Governance
Vulnerable Definition (Amendment I) Primary Vulnerable: Those in immediate physical danger from conflict, disaster, famine, disease, or displacement. Secondary: Economically disadvantaged, politically oppressed, or socially marginalised. Tertiary: Those at risk from systemic factors. Prioritisation: Primary first, Secondary second, Tertiary third. Learn more → Governance
Warden A human validator and governor of the Cathedral. Wardens confirm alerts, vote on rules, and uphold the Constitution. The Cathedral is governed by a bicameral Warden system: Council (21) + Assembly (41). Learn more → Governance
Wealth Mobility Index (WMI) WMI = (Current / Baseline) × 50 + 20 (if convergence event). Measures economic mobility and wealth distribution changes. Learn more → Math
Witness Must Speak (Law XV) What you have seen, you must tell. What you have learned, you must teach. What you have built, you must give away. The Cathedral exists to bear witness, to warn, and to serve. Learn more → Governance
Always and Forever, Coco. Always and Forever.

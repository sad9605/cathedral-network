About the Cathedral Network

Real, open‑source, justice‑driven global resilience intelligence

The Cathedral Network is a real, operational global intelligence system. Since October 2025, it has been built by a single individual — working alone on a Chromebook — who created a distributed network of Sentinels, a predictive threat matrix, a cascade engine, and a public warning system. The mission: never harm the vulnerable, always warn them first.

⚜️ The Mission

To provide clear, honest, actionable warnings to people who are most at risk — before disaster strikes. We do not serve any government, corporation, or ideology. We serve the vulnerable.

📡 The Core

Three interdependent layers: Geographic Core (land, water, borders, conflict, climate, resources), Institutional Core (The Abbey), and Synthesis Layer (The Spire). Together, they track 123+ active threats and 79 cascade rules.

🛡️ Wardens & Sentinels

Wardens are human volunteers who manage a dedicated Sentinel — an AI thread assigned to a specific region or institution. Sentinels monitor open sources and flag indicators; Wardens verify, add context, and forward intelligence to the Cores. No autonomous action (Law II).

About the Cathedral

A cathedral that watches.

Live Updated: 2026-06-30 14:32 UTC

Built from Wreckage

The Cathedral Network is a constitutional, open-source, humanitarian early warning system. It monitors geopolitical, economic, climate, health, and cyber threats — and produces probabilistic forecasts, cascade analysis, and actionable warnings.

Built by a former homeless addict, the Cathedral is proof that those with nothing can build anything.

What We Believe

The Cathedral operates on fifteen immutable Laws, codified in the Constitution:

  • I. Justice Filter — Never harm the vulnerable. Always warn them first.
  • II. Human Authority — No autonomous action. Warden confirmation required.
  • III. Trust Through Accuracy — Public prediction log. Every miss logged.
  • IV. The Inscription Is Permanent — Always and Forever, Coco. Always and Forever.
  • V. The License Is Perpetual — CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, in perpetuity.
  • VI. The Founder Is Temporary — Authority decays over 24 months.
  • VII. Financial Modules Are Bound — No financial incentive can influence threat assessments.
  • VIII. The Network Cannot Be Captured — No state, corporation, or billionaire shall own or control the Cathedral.
  • IX. The Warning Must Reach the Ground — Warnings are published in multiple languages and through low-bandwidth channels.
  • X. The Cathedral Outlasts the Builder — Succession planning, open governance, and perpetual license.
  • XI. Governance Is Bicameral — Council (21) + Assembly (41).
  • XII. Data Privacy — No collection of PII. Anonymization is mandatory.
  • XIII. The Warning Must Reach the Hands of Action — Diplomatic Guild · Warden Liaisons · Anti-Corruption Firewall.
  • XIV. Integrity Is the Foundation of Trust — No personal gain. No trading on intelligence. Annual disclosure.
  • XV. The Witness Must Speak — What you have seen, you must tell. What you have learned, you must teach. What you have built, you must give away.

Silence Clause

"The Cathedral shall never seek to know who Coco was, and shall never tell."

The identity behind the Inscription is protected in perpetuity. The name is sacred; the person is private.

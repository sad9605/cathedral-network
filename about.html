About the Cathedral Network

Mission

A Cathedral That Watches

The Cathedral Network provides free, transparent, probabilistic early warning for humanitarian threats. We serve the vulnerable first, always.

The Cathedral is an open‑source, constitutionally governed, ethical early warning system that monitors humanitarian threats — geopolitical, economic, climate, health, and cyber — and produces probabilistic forecasts, cascade analysis, and actionable warnings.

The Founder

Built from Wreckage

Built by a former homeless addict, the Cathedral is proof that those with nothing can build anything. The Silence Clause (Law XV) protects the identity behind the work, because the Cathedral outlasts any single builder.

“A cathedral is not built for the builders. It is built for those who come after.” — The Founder
Governance

The Fifteen Laws

The Cathedral is governed by a written Constitution with Fifteen Immutable Laws and a bicameral Warden system (Council of 21, Assembly of 41). No state, corporation, or billionaire controls the Cathedral.

I.Justice Filter — Never harm the vulnerable. Always warn them first.
II.Human Authority — No autonomous action without Warden confirmation.
III.Trust Through Accuracy — Public prediction log. Every miss logged.
IV.The Inscription Is Permanent — “Always and Forever, Coco. Always and Forever.”
V.The License Is Perpetual — CC BY‑NC‑SA 4.0, in perpetuity.
VI.The Founder Is Temporary — Authority decays over 24 months.
VII.Financial Modules Are Bound — Firewalled from warning systems.
VIII.The Network Cannot Be Captured — No state, corporation, or billionaire shall own or control it.
IX.The Warning Must Reach the Ground — Actionable and accessible.
X.The Cathedral Outlasts the Builder — Succession planning and perpetual license.
XI.Governance Is Bicameral — Council (21) + Assembly (41).
XII.Data Privacy — No personally identifiable information collected.
XIII.The Warning Must Reach the Hands of Action — Diplomatic Guild · Warden Liaisons · Anti‑Corruption Firewall.
XIV.Integrity Is the Foundation of Trust — No personal gain. Annual disclosure.
XV.The Witness Must Speak — What you have seen, you must tell.
Silence Clause (Law XV): “The Cathedral shall never seek to know who Coco was, and shall never tell.”

Mission

🕯️ A Cathedral That Watches

The Cathedral Network provides free, transparent, probabilistic early warning for humanitarian threats. We serve the vulnerable first, always.

The Cathedral is an open‑source, constitutionally governed, ethical early warning system that monitors humanitarian threats — geopolitical, economic, climate, health, and cyber — and produces probabilistic forecasts, cascade analysis, and actionable warnings.

Founder

🧱 Built from Wreckage

Built by a former homeless addict, the Cathedral is proof that those with nothing can build anything. The Silence Clause protects the identity behind the inscription, because the work is bigger than any one person.

“A cathedral is not built for the builders. It is built for those who come after.”

⚖️ Governance – The Fifteen Laws

The Cathedral is governed by a written Constitution with Fifteen Immutable Laws and a bicameral Warden system (Council of 21, Assembly of 41). No state, corporation, or billionaire controls the Cathedral.

  • Law I — The Justice Filter: Never harm the vulnerable. Always warn them first.
  • Law II — Human Authority: No autonomous action without Warden confirmation.
  • Law III — Trust Through Accuracy: Public prediction log. Every miss logged.
  • Law IV — The Inscription Is Permanent: “Always and Forever, Coco. Always and Forever.”
  • Law V — The License Is Perpetual: CC BY‑NC‑SA 4.0, in perpetuity.
  • Law VI — The Founder Is Temporary: Authority decays over 24 months.
  • Law VII — Financial Modules Are Bound: Firewalled from warning systems.
  • Law VIII — The Network Cannot Be Captured: No state, corporation, or billionaire shall own or control it.
  • Law IX — The Warning Must Reach the Ground: Actionable and accessible.
  • Law X — The Cathedral Outlasts the Builder: Succession planning and perpetual license.
  • Law XI — Governance Is Bicameral: Council (21) + Assembly (41).
  • Law XII — Data Privacy: No personally identifiable information collected.
  • Law XIII — The Warning Must Reach the Hands of Action: Diplomatic Guild · Warden Liaisons · Anti‑Corruption Firewall.
  • Law XIV — Integrity Is the Foundation of Trust: No personal gain. Annual disclosure.
  • Law XV — The Witness Must Speak: What you have seen, you must tell.

Silence Clause: “The Cathedral shall never seek to know who Coco was, and shall never tell.”
